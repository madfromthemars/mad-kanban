Telegram notifications

Get a Telegram message when something on the Mad Kanban board needs you, even when Obsidian is closed. Takes 1 minute.

Why Telegram? Obsidian can't send notifications to your phone: it only runs while it's open. So our board has a Telegram bot, @mad_kanban_bot, that messages you instead.

You get a message when

  • 💬 someone @mentions you in a comment
  • 💬 someone comments on a card assigned to you
  • ↩️ someone replies to your comment
  • 👤 someone assigns you to a card
  • ➡️ someone moves one of your cards to another list (e.g. To Do → In Progress)
  • 📣 Mad Kanban gets something new (a short "What's new" note, now and then)

Never about things you do yourself, and only about cards you're allowed to see.

Tip: tag teammates with @ in comments. Type @ and pick a name from the list; they get a Telegram message right away.
You needTelegram installed (phone or computer), and Mad Kanban already set up and signed in.
WhereDo it once, on any one device. It works for all your devices.

1 Open the Telegram setting

  1. In Obsidian, open Settings (gear icon ⚙; on a phone: tap ☰ at the top left, then ⚙).
  2. Tap Mad Kanban in the plugin list.
  3. Find Telegram notifications (below Your devices).

2 Connect

  1. Tap Connect Telegram. Telegram opens with the chat of Mad Kanban bot.
  2. Tap Start at the bottom of the chat.
  3. The bot answers "✅ Connected as your name".
  4. Back in Obsidian, the setting now says On for your name.
Do it within 15 minutes. The Start link works once and expires. Got "This link has expired"? Tap Connect Telegram again in Obsidian.

3 Change or turn off

I want toDo this
Stop all messagesIn Obsidian: Settings → Mad Kanban → Telegram notifications → Turn off.
Or send /stop to the bot.
Keep task messages, skip "What's new" notesSend /updates off to the bot. /updates on brings them back.
Use a different Telegram accountTap Turn off, then Connect Telegram again and press Start from the other account.
Turn it back onTap Connect Telegram again.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
No "Telegram notifications" settingUpdate Mad Kanban: Settings → Mad Kanban → Check now, then restart Obsidian. Make sure you're signed in ("Connected as" your name).
It says "Not set up on your team server yet"Tell your host.
"Connect Telegram" doesn't open TelegramInstall Telegram first. On a computer, the link may open in the browser: click Open Telegram there.
"This link has expired"Tap Connect Telegram again and press Start right away.
Connected, but no messagesMessages only come for things others do on your cards (or @mentions). Make sure you didn't send /stop or block the bot. If you blocked it, unblock it and connect again.
Too many messages/updates off stops the "What's new" notes. To stop everything: Turn off.
