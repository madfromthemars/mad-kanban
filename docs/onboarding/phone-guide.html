Mad Kanban on your phone

Set up the team board on an iPhone or Android phone. About 10 minutes, and you only do it once.

How it works. Your phone connects to the same team server as your computer. You sign in with the same account, so you see the same boards, cards, comments and assignments. A change on your phone shows up on your computer within a second, and the other way round. Your computer stays signed in.
A phoneiPhone (iOS) or Android.
Your computerAlready signed in to Mad Kanban. It shows a QR code for the phone. (No computer? Use the join link from your host.)
InternetWi-Fi or mobile data.
QR codes and join links sign in as you. Don't post them in group chats or share them with anyone. A QR code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

1 Install Obsidian

iPhoneOpen the App Store, search Obsidian and install Obsidian – Connected Notes.
AndroidOpen Google Play, search Obsidian and install Obsidian.

Both are free and made by Dynalist Inc.

2 Create a vault

Obsidian keeps everything in a folder called a vault.

  1. Open Obsidian and tap Create a vault.
  2. Name it Mad Kanban.
  3. iPhone: turn off "Store in iCloud". You don't need it, because the board syncs through our server.
    Android: keep the default location. If the phone asks for storage access, tap Allow.
  4. Tap Create.

3 Turn on community plugins

  1. Open the side menu: tap ☰ at the top left, or swipe right from the left edge of the screen.
  2. Tap the gear icon ⚙ to open Settings.
  3. Tap Community plugins, then Turn on community plugins.

4 Install BRAT

BRAT is a helper plugin from the official plugin store. It installs Mad Kanban for you.

  1. Still in Settings → Community plugins, tap Browse.
  2. Search BRAT. Open BRAT by TfTHacker.
  3. Tap Install, then Enable.

5 Install Mad Kanban with BRAT

  1. Go back to Settings. Scroll down the left list to the plugin section and tap BRAT.
  2. Tap Add beta plugin.
  3. In the box, type exactly: madfromthemars/mad-kanban
  4. Leave the version on the latest one. Make sure Enable after installing the plugin is ticked.
  5. Tap Add plugin and wait a few seconds until BRAT says it's installed.
Mad Kanban doesn't show up? Go to Settings → Community plugins, find Mad Kanban under Installed plugins and switch it on. If it isn't in the list, close Obsidian completely and open it again.

6 Sign in: scan the QR code

  1. On your computer, in Obsidian: Settings → Mad Kanban → Add a device. A QR code appears.
  2. On the phone, open the Camera and point it at the QR code. Tap the link that pops up.
  3. A page opens. Tap Open in Obsidian, then Sign in.
  4. Your computer says "… is signed in". Done. The phone has its own sign-in, and your computer stays signed in.
No computer, or the camera doesn't work? Get the link onto the phone (the easiest way is Telegram → Saved Messages), copy it, then in Obsidian on the phone go to Settings → Mad Kanban and paste it into Team server join string. This works with the QR link and with the join link from your host. Below the field it should say Connected as and your name.

7 Open the team board

  1. Close Settings and open the side menu (tap ☰ or swipe right).
  2. Tap the board icon named Open a team board.
    Can't find it? Swipe down in the middle of the screen to open the command list and type Open a team board.
  3. Pick the board, for example Dragonfly. Next time, just tap it in the file list.

Done. Your phone and computer now show the same board.

Using the board on a phone

To do thisDo this
Open a cardTap it. You'll see the description, checklist, comments and attachments.
Move a cardPress and hold the card, then drag it to another column. Swipe sideways to reach the other columns.
Add a cardTap + Add a card under a column.
Check syncThe cloud at the top left: green synced  yellow saving  orange offline  red error. Tap it to sync again.

Updates

Mad Kanban updates itself, so you don't have to do anything. To check right away: Settings → Mad Kanban → Check now.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
BRAT says it can't find the pluginCheck the spelling: madfromthemars/mad-kanban, with no spaces. Check your internet.
"That does not look like a join string"Part of the link is missing. Copy the whole link again.
"This code is invalid, used or expired"A QR code works once, for 10 minutes. On your computer tap New code and scan again.
"Open in Obsidian" does nothingMad Kanban isn't installed on the phone yet (steps 3–5), or use the paste method in step 6.
"Connection failed"Check your internet. If it still fails, tell your host. The server may be down.
No boards, or the board is emptyYou haven't been added to a project yet. Ask your host.
Lost your phoneOn your computer: Settings → Mad Kanban → Your devices → Remove next to the phone. Only the phone is signed out. No computer at hand? Ask your host to remove it in Manage team → Devices.
