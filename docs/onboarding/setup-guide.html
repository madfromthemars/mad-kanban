Mad Kanban: Setup Guide

From zero to working on the team board, on your computer and your phone. About 15 minutes, no experience needed.

What this is. Our tasks live on a shared board, like sticky notes on a wall. Each task is a card, and cards move through To Do → In Progress → Done, with Archive for finished cards worth keeping. The board runs inside Obsidian, a free app, and stays in sync for everyone: a change shows up on every screen within a second.

Every card belongs to a project. You see only the projects you've been added to, so your board may look different from someone else's.

Part A · Computer

1 Install Obsidian

  1. Open obsidian.md/download in your browser and download the version for your computer.
  2. Windows: run the downloaded .exe. Mac: open the .dmg and drag Obsidian into Applications.

2 Create a vault

Obsidian keeps everything in a folder called a vault.

  1. Start Obsidian. Next to Create new vault, click Create.
  2. Name it Mad Kanban, choose where to keep it (for example Documents), and click Create.

3 Install Mad Kanban

Mad Kanban isn't in Obsidian's plugin store. A helper plugin called BRAT (which is in the store) installs it for you.

  1. Click the gear icon ⚙ at the bottom left to open Settings.
  2. Click Community plugins, then Turn on community plugins.
  3. Click Browse, search BRAT, open BRAT by TfTHacker, then click Install and Enable.
  4. Close the store window. In the Settings list on the left, scroll down to the plugins section and click BRAT.
  5. Click Add beta plugin and type exactly: madfromthemars/mad-kanban
  6. Leave the version on the latest one, make sure Enable after installing the plugin is ticked, and click Add plugin.
Mad Kanban not showing up? Go to Settings → Community plugins, find Mad Kanban under Installed plugins and switch it on. Still not there? Quit Obsidian completely and start it again.

4 Sign in with your join link

  1. In Settings, click Mad Kanban in the plugin list on the left.
  2. Paste your join link into Team server join string.
  3. Below it, you should see Connected as and your name. If not, click Test connection.

You only need the join link once. After that, Obsidian keeps you signed in.

5 Open the team board

  1. Close Settings. In the thin icon bar on the far left, click the board icon (it says Open a team board when you point at it).
  2. Pick the board, for example Dragonfly. It opens and appears in your file list, so next time just click it there.

A team board on the computer.

Your computer is set up. Want the board on your phone too? Keep the computer open and go on to Part B.
Part B · Phone iPhone or Android

The phone signs in as you by scanning a QR code from your computer. You don't need your join link for this.

6 Install Obsidian and create a vault

iPhoneIn the App Store, install Obsidian – Connected Notes. Open it, tap Create a vault, name it Mad Kanban, and turn off "Store in iCloud". Tap Create.
AndroidIn Google Play, install Obsidian. Open it, tap Create a vault, name it Mad Kanban, and keep the default location. Allow storage access if asked. Tap Create.

7 Install Mad Kanban on the phone

  1. Open the side menu: tap ☰ at the top left, or swipe right from the left edge. Tap the gear icon ⚙.
  2. Community plugins → Turn on community plugins → Browse. Search BRAT, install it and tap Enable.
  3. Back in Settings, tap BRAT → Add beta plugin, type madfromthemars/mad-kanban, keep Enable after installing ticked, and tap Add plugin.

8 Sign in: scan the QR code

  1. On your computer: Settings → Mad Kanban → Add a device. A QR code appears.
  2. On the phone: open the Camera, point it at the QR code, and tap the link that pops up.
  3. A page opens. Tap Open in Obsidian, then Sign in.
  4. Your computer says "… is signed in". The phone has its own sign-in, and the computer stays signed in.
Camera doesn't work, or no computer at hand? Get the link onto the phone (e.g. send it to yourself in Telegram → Saved Messages), copy it, then in Obsidian on the phone go to Settings → Mad Kanban and paste it into Team server join string. This works with the QR link and with the join link from your host. A QR code works once and expires after 10 minutes; tap New code on the computer for another.

9 Open the team board on the phone

  1. Open the side menu and tap the board icon (Open a team board). Can't find it? Swipe down in the middle of the screen and type Open a team board.
  2. Pick Dragonfly. Next time, just tap it in the file list.
Done. Your computer and phone show the same board, and changes on one appear on the other within a second. The plugin updates itself on both, so you never have to reinstall it.

Part D · Devices and problems

Your devices

Settings → Mad Kanban → Your devices lists every computer and phone signed in as you, with when each was last used. Each one has its own sign-in, so you can rename or Remove one without touching the others. Add a device shows a new QR code whenever you get a new phone or computer.

Lost your phone? On your computer: Settings → Mad Kanban → Your devices → Remove next to the phone. Only the phone is signed out. No computer at hand? Ask your host to remove it in Manage team → Devices.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
BRAT can't find the pluginCheck the spelling: madfromthemars/mad-kanban, no spaces. Check your internet.
Mad Kanban isn't in the Settings listSettings → Community plugins → switch Mad Kanban on. Then restart Obsidian.
"That does not look like a join string"Part of the link is missing. Copy the whole link again.
"Connection failed"Check your internet. If it still fails, tell your host; the server may be down.
"This code is invalid, used or expired"A QR code works once, for 10 minutes. On the computer tap New code and scan again.
"Open in Obsidian" does nothingMad Kanban isn't installed on the phone yet (step 7), or paste the link instead (box under step 8).
"This device was signed out"It was removed from your devices, or your link was replaced. Sign in again with Add a device from another device, or a new link from your host.
No boards, or an empty boardYou haven't been added to a project yet. Ask your host.
