- **Board cards on canvases.** In a canvas, click the new board icon at the end of the bottom toolbar and pick a card. You can also drag a card from the board onto a canvas, or type `@` in a canvas box. The box shows the card's title, list and people, and stays up to date. Click the title to open the card.
- **Advanced Canvas is now part of the team setup.** If you don't have it yet, Mad Kanban shows an **Install Advanced Canvas** button. One click installs it and turns it on. Shapes and arrow styles on shared canvases now show the same for everyone.
