- **@mentions in comments.** Type @ and pick a teammate from the list. They see it highlighted and get a Telegram message.
- **Telegram notifications.** Settings → Mad Kanban → Telegram notifications → Connect Telegram. You get a message when someone @mentions you, comments on your cards, assigns you, or moves one of your cards, even when Obsidian is closed.
- **What's new notes** like this one, whenever Mad Kanban gets something new. Send /updates off to the bot to stop them.
