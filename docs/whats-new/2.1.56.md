**Phone board redesigned**
- Lists are now **tabs at the top** (To Do, In Progress, Done…) with card counts. Tap a tab to switch.
- To move a card to another list: **hold the card and drop it on a tab**.
- Cards are compact rows: priority, title, due date and checklist on one line, avatar on the right.
- Add a card with the round **+** button at the bottom right. List options are under **⋯** next to the tabs.
- Changing a card's priority no longer jumps it around the list; lists re-sort when you open the board or change a filter.
