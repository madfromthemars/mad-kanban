- **Time estimates.** Open a card and set **Estimate** (e.g. `2h`, `1d`, or tap a quick button). The card then shows real time against the estimate, like `⏱ 3h / 2h`. Real time only counts while the card is in **In Progress** or Review. The chip turns yellow when a card goes over.
- **Phone: swipe between lists.** Swipe left or right on the board to go to the next or previous list. Swipe past the first list to open the left sidebar, and past the last list to open the right one.
- **Phone: paste pictures.** Copy an image and paste it into a card's description or a comment.
- **Old Done cards tidy themselves.** Cards that have been in Done for more than 7 days are archived automatically, which keeps the board short. Nothing is deleted.
- The 🗑 button on the board opens the **Trash**, where you can restore cards deleted in the last 30 days.
