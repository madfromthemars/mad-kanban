- **Link one card from another.** Type `@` in a card's description, a checklist item or a comment, then pick a card from the list. It shows as a small chip with the card's title and list; tap it to open that card. Great for "Blocked by:" lines.
- Done cards show crossed out in the chip, so you can see when a blocker is finished.
- A card that others point to shows **Mentioned in** in its window.
