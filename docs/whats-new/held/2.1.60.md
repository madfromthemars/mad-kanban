🔑 **Team passwords.** Keep logins and API keys in Mad Kanban, per project. Command palette → "Passwords".
- Every password is shared only with the people you pick. Nobody else sees it, or even that it exists — not even the owner.
- Locked on your device before it's sent: the server can't read it.
- Copy clears from the clipboard after 30 seconds. History shows who opened or copied what.
- First time: "Set up passwords" and save your recovery code somewhere safe.
- Phone or second computer: sign it in with "Add a device" (Settings → Mad Kanban) and your key comes along. Already signed in? Open Passwords there and tap "Ask my other device".

🧭 **Canvas links in shared notes** now work for everyone, and you're told when a canvas links to notes your team doesn't have (with a button to share them).
