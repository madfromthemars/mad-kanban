🔑 **Team passwords.** Keep logins and API keys in Mad Kanban, per project.
- Every project folder in Team/ now has a **Passwords** file (with a key icon). Click it to see that project's passwords.
- Each password is shared only with the people you pick. Nobody else sees it, or even that it exists, not even the owner. You can see who has it by the small initials on each row.
- Passwords are locked on your device before they're sent, so the server can't read them.
- Copied passwords clear from the clipboard after 30 seconds. History shows who opened or copied what.
- First time: open Passwords → "Set up passwords", and save your recovery code somewhere safe.
- Phone or second computer: sign it in with "Add a device" (Settings → Mad Kanban) and your key comes along. Already signed in there? Open Passwords and tap "Ask my other device".

🗂 **Icons in the file list:** team boards, shared notes, canvases and attachments now have their own icons, so you can see at a glance what's shared.

🧭 **Canvas links in shared notes** now work for everyone, and you're told when a canvas links to notes your team doesn't have (with a button to share them).
