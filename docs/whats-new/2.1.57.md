**Phone: new card window and filters**
- The card window has a top bar: **✕**, the card title, and **⋯** (copy ID, archive, delete).
- Status, priority and due date are buttons right under the title. Other fields are under **Details**.
- The comment box is one row with a send button. A checklist item's ✕ only shows while you edit it.
- **Filters** open from the bottom: tap **Only my cards**, a person, a project, a priority or a tag.
- The **+** to add a card is next to the list tabs.
